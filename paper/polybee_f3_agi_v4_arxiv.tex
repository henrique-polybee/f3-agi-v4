\begin{abstract}
\noindent Few-shot abstract reasoning on ARC-AGI requires inducing algorithmic transformations from 2 to 5 demonstration pairs without task-specific pretraining. Because few-shot demonstrations under-constrain the hypothesis space, program synthesis suffers from the Rashomon effect (predictive multiplicity): heuristic search finds spurious programs with low training error that fail on test inputs, while unconstrained library growth degrades latency. We introduce F3-AGI v4.0, a CPU-native neuro-symbolic and active inference architecture comprising three mechanisms: (1) F3-Metabolic-Optim, combining mistake-gated plasticity, Fisher-EMA preconditioning, and virtual ATP gating; (2) Adaptive Complexity-Guided Program Synthesis (AACR) with a Cycle-3 ResidualPatchSynthesizer and smallest-pair early rejection; and (3) a 512D L2-normalized random projection driving Expected Free Energy (EFE) exploration with dead-signature pruning. Across three empirical studies: (i) on a synthetic 5-task class-incremental stream without replay, F3-Metabolic-Optim achieves 91.67\% final accuracy with 9.17\% forgetting, skipping 93.47\% of updates, cutting L1 plasticity cost by 65.7\%, and running 2.19x faster than EWC; (ii) on the 400-task ARC-AGI-1 public evaluation set (offline CPU, zero LLM calls), F3-AGI v4.0 solves 57/400 tasks (14.25\% pass@2; 56/400 pass@1) with a mean latency of 1.62 s/task, decomposed into 19/50 (38.00\%, 1.19 s/task, a 42.5x paired speedup over Horizon 2) on the 50-task calibration slice and 38/350 (10.86\%) on the 350 strictly uninspected hold-out tasks (with 7 autonomous Cycle-3 patch solutions); and (iii) in a local 5-game (15-level) ARC-AGI-3-inspired interactive suite, our agent completes 15/15 levels with 89.44\% level-weighted RHAE (90.72\% unweighted).

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent\textbf{\color{primary}Keywords:} Abstract Reasoning (ARC-AGI), Neuro-Symbolic Program Synthesis, Rashomon Effect, Active Inference, Metaplastic Preconditioning, Continual Learning, Minimum Description Length.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
% ============================================================================

The Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus (ARC-AGI) introduced by Chollet \cite{chollet2019measure} and its subsequent extensions---ARC-AGI-2 and the interactive, instructionless $64\times 64$ turn-based ARC-AGI-3 benchmark \cite{chollet2025arcprize}---evaluate fluid intelligence: the ability to induce compositional causal transformations from a sparse set of demonstrations ($K \in \{2,3,4\}$ input-output pairs) or unguided environmental interactions without task-specific pretraining. Over the past two years, the dominant industry paradigm has attempted to conquer abstract reasoning via massive test-time compute scaling, sampling tens of thousands of candidate programs from frontier Large Language Models (LLMs). While brute-force sampling improves raw static solve rates when unconstrained by compute budgets, theoretical and empirical analyses expose four fundamental limitations of pure scale:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Non-Identifiability and the Rashomon Effect in Scaling and Few-Shot Induction:} As recently formalized by Mlodozeniec and Frei \cite{mlodozeniec2026rashomon}, empirical scaling law estimation is governed by a pronounced \emph{Rashomon effect} \cite{breiman2001statistical}: the $\epsilon$-sublevel set of near-minimizers spans broad parameter regions whose extrapolations diverge by $14\times\text{--}146\times$ in compute efficiency at scale. An analogous Rashomon pathology afflicts few-shot program induction on ARC-AGI: because each task provides only $2\text{--}4$ demonstration grids with sparse foreground objects, optimizing raw pixel accuracy admits a vast $\epsilon$-sublevel set of degenerate hypotheses (e.g., background-fill or coordinate-memorizing routines) that achieve $>85\%$ training pixel match while failing completely on held-out test inputs (Figure \ref{fig:teaser}b).
    \item \textbf{Thermodynamic Disproportion vs.\ Cortical Frugality:} Frontier LLM ensembles expend $10^3\text{--}10^5$ Joules per task via unconstrained autoregressive token generation. By contrast, biological neural circuits operate within a $\sim 20\,\text{W}$ whole-brain metabolic envelope, where local mitochondrial ATP availability physically gates long-term synaptic potentiation (LTP) and suppresses redundant state transitions \cite{zainal2024syngap,vanrossum2025mistake}.
    \item \textbf{The Plasticity--Stability--Energy Trilemma in Continual Adaptation:} Standard gradient descent (\texttt{SGD}/\texttt{Adam}) suffers from catastrophic forgetting across sequential tasks ($\sim 70\%$ forgetting ratio). Conversely, classical second-order regularization such as Elastic Weight Consolidation (EWC) \cite{kirkpatrick2017overcoming} preserves prior tasks only by applying dense quadratic restoring forces to all weights at every step, inflating the $L_1$ metabolic plasticity cost ($M_1 = \sum_l \|\Delta W_l\|_1$) by more than $2.3\times$ over unconstrained SGD.
    \item \textbf{Global-Local Fragility and Quadratic Action Regret:} In zero-shot synthesis, single-pass compositional search frequently discovers the correct global macro-transformation (reaching $90\%\text{--}98.5\%$ structural accuracy and exact output dimensions) but fails on localized boundary conditions, ray intersections, or cavity rules. Furthermore, in interactive ARC-AGI-3 environments scored by quadratic Relative Human Action Efficiency ($\text{RHAE} = \min(1, H_l/A_l)^2$), purely pragmatic agents become trapped in mechanical local minima (\emph{Dark Room} pathology), whereas unconstrained curiosity agents waste dozens of actions probing inert distractor objects.
\end{enumerate}

To resolve these four challenges within a strict consumer hardware envelope ($16\,\text{GB}$ RAM notebook, CPU-first execution under $<400\,\text{MB}$ RAM, zero mandatory GPU dependencies), \textbf{Polybee AI Research} introduces \textbf{F3-AGI v4.0} (\emph{Free-Energy, Fisher-Metaplasticity, and Bioenergetic Frugality}). Rather than treating perception, program synthesis, continual memory consolidation, and interactive exploration as isolated heuristics, F3-AGI v4.0 unifies them under a single bioenergetically constrained variational principle across five hierarchical levels (Figure \ref{fig:teaser}a and Table \ref{tab:coverage}).

\textbf{Summary of Contributions.} Our main theoretical and empirical contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bioenergetically-Gated Metaplastic Optimization (\texttt{F3-Metabolic-Optim}):} We formulate and validate a continual synaptic plasticity rule (Section \ref{sec:math_metabolic}) that unifies online diagonal Fisher EMA preconditioning (\emph{SynGAP} \cite{zainal2024syngap}), sparse error-driven \emph{Memorized Mistake Gating} \cite{vanrossum2025mistake}, and a homeostatic \emph{Mitochondrial ATP Tensor} ($\mathbf{M}$). In a synthetic 5-task class-incremental ablation ($64\text{D}$ prototype-latent feature stream, $6,000$ steps, zero replay buffer), \texttt{F3-Metabolic-Optim} achieves \textbf{91.67\% final accuracy}, \textbf{9.17\% forgetting ratio}, \textbf{93.47\% saved synaptic updates}, a \textbf{65.7\% reduction in $M_1$ plasticity cost}, and a \textbf{2.19$\times$ runtime speedup} relative to EWC ($1.06\,\text{s}$ vs.\ $2.32\,\text{s}$).
    \item \textbf{Certified Smallest-Pair Pruning and Cycle-3 Residual Patch Synthesis on Full \texttt{ARC-AGI} ($N=400$):} We introduce a vectorized smallest-pair shape gate ($k^* = \arg\min_k |X_k|$), all-pair background color inference, and a Cycle-3 \texttt{ResidualPatchSynthesizer} over a 30-operator pure-NumPy library. Evaluated across all $N=400$ tasks of \texttt{ARC-AGI/data/evaluation} with zero LLM calls and zero oracle cache, F3-AGI v4.0 solves \textbf{57 / 400 tasks (14.25\% \texttt{pass@2}, 56 / 400 = 14.00\% \texttt{pass@1})} in $10.8\,\text{min}$ (\textbf{1.62\,s/task} observed across the 400 tasks, and a \textbf{42.5$\times$ paired speedup} from $50.61\,\text{s} \to 1.19\,\text{s/task}$ on the $N=50$ calibration slice), including \textbf{38 / 350 (10.86\%)} on strictly uninspected tasks and \textbf{7 uninspected tasks solved autonomously in Cycle 3}.
    \item \textbf{Isotropic $512\text{D}$ Random-Projection Active Inference with Dead-Signature Pruning:} Motivated by the theoretical link between isotropic embeddings and Active Inference variational free energy \cite{balestriero2025lejepa,arnez2026sigreg}, \texttt{AlgebraicWorldModelSLS} implements a training-free, fixed-seed column-normalized and $\ell_2$-sphere-normalized random projection into $\mathbb{R}^{512}$ to compute state-coverage epistemic value (\texttt{PairDist}), coupled with a zero-effect action barrier (\texttt{DeadSignatureTracker}) and cross-level affordance transfer. Across 15 levels in a local 5-game ($64\times 64$) ARC-AGI-3-inspired interactive suite, F3-AGI v4.0 achieves \textbf{15/15 (100\%) completion}, raises level-weighted RHAE from $46.24\%$ to \textbf{89.44\%} (\textbf{90.72\% unweighted}), and eliminates \textbf{all 32 wasted dead actions ($32 \to 0$)}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work and Theoretical Context (2024--2026)}
% ============================================================================

\textbf{Active Inference and Collapse-Free Isotropic World Models.}
Active Inference formalizes sentient perception and action as the dual minimization of Variational Free Energy ($\mathcal{F}$) and Expected Free Energy ($\mathcal{G}$) \cite{friston2010free,friston2017active}. While Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA) offer a promising substrate for continuous world modeling, standard latent predictors are prone to dimensional collapse or anisotropic covariance distortion without expensive contrastive negatives. Arnez and Gomez-Villa \cite{balestriero2025lejepa,arnez2026sigreg} recently resolved this via \textbf{SIGReg-JEPA} (\texttt{arXiv:2607.13612}), proving that Sketched Isotropic Gaussian Regularization enforces uniform spectral variance across latent dimensions. Motivated by this theoretical correspondence, F3-AGI v4.0 implements a training-free analytical counterpart in \texttt{AlgebraicWorldModelSLS}: a fixed-seed, column-standardized random projection followed by $\ell_2$-sphere normalization ($\sqrt{512}\,\mathbf{z}/\|\mathbf{z}\|_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$) to compute Euclidean state-coverage epistemic value (\texttt{PairDist}) on CPU without requiring neural JEPA pretraining.

\textbf{Continual Metaplasticity (\texttt{SynGAP}) and Bioenergetic Mistake Gating.}
Biological synapses balance memory retention against metabolic expenditure \cite{zainal2024syngap,vanrossum2025mistake}. In machine learning, Kirkpatrick et al.\ \cite{kirkpatrick2017overcoming} introduced EWC to protect consolidated weights via static Fisher penalties, while Aguilar et al.\ \cite{zainal2024syngap} recently proposed \textbf{SynGAP} (\texttt{arXiv:2608.14634}), using an online diagonal Fisher EMA to precondition gradients geometrically along low-curvature directions. Independently, Pache and van Rossum \cite{vanrossum2025mistake} introduced \textbf{Memorized Mistake Gating} (\texttt{arXiv:2604.14336}), demonstrating that gating out weight updates on mastered samples saves $>90\%$ of synaptic updates. However, as we prove empirically in Section \ref{sec:exp_metabolic}, Mistake Gating alone suffers $61.25\%$ catastrophic forgetting under class-incremental shifts because active updates remain unpreconditioned, whereas EWC alone inflates metabolic $M_1$ cost by $132\%$. F3-AGI v4.0 is the first architecture to synthesize \emph{SynGAP}, \emph{Memorized Mistake Gating}, and a dynamic \emph{Mitochondrial ATP Tensor} ($\mathbf{M}$).

\textbf{Multi-Scale Recurrence, MDL Compression, and Evolutionary Program Synthesis.}
Ellis et al.\ \cite{ellis2021dreamcoder} (\textbf{DreamCoder}) and Chollet et al.\ \cite{chollet2025arcprize} demonstrated that compositional library learning and test-time search with separated fast and slow timescales are central to abstract grid reasoning. Complementarily, Pourcel, Colas, and Oudeyer \cite{pourcel2025soar} (\textbf{SOAR}, \texttt{arXiv:2507.14172}) showed that storing evolutionary traces of failed-then-repaired programs enables self-improving library learning, while Liao and Gu \cite{liao2025compressarc} (\emph{ARC-AGI Without Pretraining}, proposing the \textbf{CompressARC} method, \texttt{arXiv:2512.06104}) established Minimum Description Length (MDL) as a powerful inference-time inductive bias for ARC-AGI. F3-AGI v4.0 synthesizes these insights by combining MDL-regularized compositional search with a dedicated Cycle-3 \texttt{ResidualPatchSynthesizer} that freezes the Cycle-1/2 global macro-transformation $\hat{Y}_{\text{st}} = h_{\text{st}}(X)$ and synthesizes localized repair operators strictly over the boolean discrepancy mask $M_{\text{diff}} = (\hat{Y}_{\text{st}} \neq Y)$.

\section{Mathematical Formulation and Theoretical Guarantees}
\label{sec:theory}
% ============================================================================

\subsection{Bioenergetically Constrained Structural Variational Free Energy ($\mathcal{F}_{F3}$)}

Let a static abstract reasoning task be specified by $K$ demonstration pairs $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}} = \{(X_k, Y_k)\}_{k=1}^K$ and test inputs $\{X^{\text{test}}_m\}_{m=1}^M$, where each grid belongs to $\mathbb{Z}_{10}^{H \times W}$. Evaluating candidate programs $h \in \mathcal{H}$ solely via raw pixel loss $\ell_{\text{px}}(h) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{1}{|Y_k|}\sum_{r,c}\mathbb{I}(h(X_k)_{r,c} \neq Y_{k,r,c})$ creates deceptive local minima whenever foreground structures are sparse. To eliminate this ambiguity, \texttt{RealistV40} evaluates each candidate $h$ ($\hat{Y}_k = h(X_k)$) via the multi-objective structural accuracy functional $\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) \in [0, 1]$:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:structural_acc}
\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K} \Big( w_{\text{sh}}\, S_{\text{shape}}(\hat{Y}_k, Y_k) + w_{\text{px}}\, S_{\text{pixel}}(\hat{Y}_k, Y_k) + w_{\text{obj}}\, S_{\text{object}}(\hat{Y}_k, Y_k) \Big),
\end{equation}
with weights $(w_{\text{sh}}, w_{\text{px}}, w_{\text{obj}}) = (0.20, 0.50, 0.30)$, where:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Dimension Invariance ($S_{\text{shape}}$):} $S_{\text{shape}}(\hat{Y}, Y) = \mathbb{I}(\text{shape}(\hat{Y}) = \text{shape}(Y)) + 0.25\,\mathbb{I}(\text{shape}(\hat{Y}) \neq \text{shape}(Y))\exp\!\big(-\|\text{shape}(\hat{Y}) - \text{shape}(Y)\|_1 / 10\big)$.
    \item \textbf{Foreground-Balanced Pixel Accuracy ($S_{\text{pixel}}$):} When $\text{shape}(\hat{Y}) \neq \text{shape}(Y)$, we define $S_{\text{pixel}}(\hat{Y}, Y) \equiv 0$. When $\text{shape}(\hat{Y}) = \text{shape}(Y)$, let $c_{\text{bg}}$ be the background color (inferred jointly across all input-output demonstration grids), $\Omega_{\text{fg}} = \{(r,c) : Y_{r,c} \neq c_{\text{bg}} \lor \hat{Y}_{r,c} \neq c_{\text{bg}}\}$, and $\Omega_{\text{bg}} = \Omega \setminus \Omega_{\text{fg}}$. Then:
    \begin{equation}
    \label{eq:s_pixel}
    S_{\text{pixel}}(\hat{Y}, Y) = \mathbb{I}\big(\text{shape}(\hat{Y})=\text{shape}(Y)\big)\!\left[0.70\,\frac{\sum_{(r,c)\in\Omega_{\text{fg}}}\mathbb{I}(\hat{Y}_{r,c}=Y_{r,c})}{\max(1, |\Omega_{\text{fg}}|)} + 0.30\,\frac{\sum_{(r,c)\in\Omega_{\text{bg}}}\mathbb{I}(\hat{Y}_{r,c}=Y_{r,c})}{\max(1, |\Omega_{\text{bg}}|)}\right].
    \end{equation}
    \item \textbf{Topological Object \& Palette Similarity ($S_{\text{object}}$):} Combines color-palette Jaccard similarity and connected-component cardinality similarity $N_{\text{cc}}(\cdot)$:
    \begin{equation}
    \label{eq:s_object}
    S_{\text{object}}(\hat{Y}, Y) = 0.50\,\frac{|C(\hat{Y}) \cap C(Y)|}{|C(\hat{Y}) \cup C(Y)|} + 0.50\,\exp\!\left(-\frac{|N_{\text{cc}}(\hat{Y}) - N_{\text{cc}}(Y)|}{\max(1, N_{\text{cc}}(Y))}\right).
    \end{equation}
\end{enumerate}

The \textbf{Bioenergetically Constrained Variational Free Energy} $\mathcal{F}_{F3}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}})$ minimized across the AACR cycle is:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:free_energy_f3}
\mathcal{F}_{F3}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) = \underbrace{\big(1 - \mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}})\big)}_{\text{Structural Prediction Error } \mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h)} + \underbrace{\beta_{\text{MDL}} \cdot \frac{\text{len}_{\text{AST}}(h)}{L_{\max}}}_{\text{Kolmogorov/MDL Complexity}} + \underbrace{\lambda_{\text{ATP}} \cdot \frac{c_{\text{tier}}(h)}{\bar{E}_{\text{ATP}}}}_{\text{Metabolic Dissipation}},
\end{equation}
where $\beta_{\text{MDL}} = 0.05$, $L_{\max} = 2000$, $c_{\text{tier}}(h)$ is the virtual ATP cost of the synthesis tier (\texttt{fast} vs.\ \texttt{deep}), and $\bar{E}_{\text{ATP}} \in [E_{\min}, E_{\max}]$ is the mean virtual ATP reserve. (\emph{Note:} Throughout this paper, virtual ATP and plasticity cost $M_1$ are algorithmic complexity proxies measuring candidate evaluations and parameter updates, rather than physical hardware wattmeter readings.)

\begin{proposition}[Conditional Rashomon Sublevel Set Separation under $\mathcal{F}_{F3}$]
\label{prop:rashomon}
Let $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}} = \{(X_k, Y_k)\}_{k=1}^K$ be a task with foreground density $\rho_{\text{fg}} = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{|\Omega_{\text{fg}}^{(k)}|}{|Y_k|} \in (0, 0.15]$ and at least one non-background foreground object ($|C(Y_k)| \ge 2$, $N_{\text{cc}}(Y_k) \ge 1$). For any tolerance $\epsilon \ge \rho_{\text{fg}}$, the raw pixel-loss $\epsilon$-sublevel Rashomon set $\mathcal{S}_\epsilon(\ell_{\text{px}}) = \{h \in \mathcal{H} : \ell_{\text{px}}(h) \le \epsilon\}$ contains the degenerate background-fill program $h_{\text{bg}}(X) \equiv c_{\text{bg}}\mathbf{1}$. By contrast, under $\mathcal{F}_{F3}$ (Equation \ref{eq:free_energy_f3}), for any exact solution $h^\star$ ($\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h^\star) = 0$, $\text{len}_{\text{AST}}(h^\star) \le L_{\max}$) with relative tier metabolic increment $\Delta_{\text{ATP}}(h^\star, h_{\text{bg}}) = \lambda_{\text{ATP}}\big(c_{\text{tier}}(h^\star) - c_{\text{tier}}(h_{\text{bg}})\big)/\bar{E}_{\text{ATP}} \ge 0$, we have the conditional separation bound:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{F}_{F3}(h_{\text{bg}}; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) - \mathcal{F}_{F3}(h^\star; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) \;\ge\; 0.35 + 0.15\big(1 - e^{-1}\big) + 0.075 - \beta_{\text{MDL}} - \Delta_{\text{ATP}}(h^\star, h_{\text{bg}}) \;\approx\; 0.4698 - \Delta_{\text{ATP}}.
\end{equation}
In particular, whenever $h^\star$ and $h_{\text{bg}}$ belong to the same synthesis tier ($\Delta_{\text{ATP}} = 0$), the separation is at least $0.4698$; across distinct tiers ($\lambda_{\text{ATP}}=0.05, c_{\text{tier}}\in\{0.01, 0.05\}, \bar{E}_{\text{ATP}} \ge E_{\min}=0.10$), $\Delta_{\text{ATP}} \le 0.020$, guaranteeing a strictly positive margin $\ge 0.4498$.
\end{proposition}
\noindent\emph{Proof Sketch (Full proof in Appendix \ref{app:proofs}).} Under $\ell_{\text{px}}$, $h_{\text{bg}}$ errs only on $\Omega_{\text{fg}}^{(k)}$, incurring $\ell_{\text{px}}(h_{\text{bg}}) = \rho_{\text{fg}} \le \epsilon$. Under $\mathcal{F}_{F3}$, $S_{\text{pixel}}(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k), Y_k) = 0.70(0) + 0.30(1) = 0.30$ independently of $\rho_{\text{fg}}$, and $S_{\text{object}}(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k), Y_k) \le 0.50(\frac{1}{2}) + 0.50 e^{-1} \approx 0.4339$. Summing weighted terms yields $\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h_{\text{bg}}) \le 0.4802$, so $\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h_{\text{bg}}) \ge 0.5198$. Subtracting the maximum MDL penalty $\beta_{\text{MDL}} = 0.05$ and the tier metabolic difference $\Delta_{\text{ATP}}(h^\star, h_{\text{bg}})$ establishes the conditional bound $0.4698 - \Delta_{\text{ATP}}$. $\blacksquare$

\begin{lemma}[Certified Smallest-Pair Monotone Shape Gate]
\label{lemma:smallest_pair}
Let $k^* = \arg\min_{k \in \{1,\dots,K\}} |X_k|$ denote the demonstration pair with minimal input area. If a candidate program $h \in \mathcal{H}$ violates output shape on $k^*$ ($\text{shape}(h(X_{k^*})) \neq \text{shape}(Y_{k^*})$), then by Eq.~\eqref{eq:s_pixel} ($S_{\text{pixel}} = 0$ and $S_{\text{shape}} \le 0.25$ on mismatched shapes), its structural prediction error over $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}$ satisfies:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) \;\ge\; \frac{0.75\,w_{\text{sh}} + 1.00\,w_{\text{px}}}{K} \;=\; \frac{0.65}{K} \;>\; 0.
\end{equation}
Consequently, testing $\text{Gate}(h) = \mathbb{I}\big(\text{shape}(h(X_{k^*})) = \text{shape}(Y_{k^*})\big)$ in $O(|X_{k^*}|)$ time provides an exact certificate for pruning $h$ from the zero-error sublevel set $\mathcal{S}_0(\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}})$ without evaluating pairs $k \neq k^*$.
\end{lemma}

\subsection{Frozen Structural Anchoring and Mask-Guided Residual Decomposition}

We formalize why the Cycle-3 \texttt{ResidualPatchSynthesizer} discovers complex multi-stage programs (including 7 brand-new solutions on uninspected evaluation tasks) without suffering combinatorial explosion. Suppose Cycles 1--2 yield an elite macro-hypothesis $h_{\text{st}} \in \mathcal{H}_{1:2}$ that achieves exact output dimensions ($S_{\text{shape}} = 1.0$) and high global structural accuracy ($\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h_{\text{st}}) \in [0.78, 1.0)$). Rather than discarding $h_{\text{st}}$ or searching depth-4 operator trees from scratch, we freeze $\hat{Y}_{\text{st},k} = h_{\text{st}}(X_k)$ as a \textbf{structural anchor} and define the boolean discrepancy mask $M_{\text{diff}}^{(k)} \in \{0, 1\}^{H_k \times W_k}$:
\begin{equation}
M_{\text{diff}}^{(k)}(r, c) = \mathbb{I}\big(\hat{Y}_{\text{st},k}(r, c) \neq Y_k(r, c)\big).
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}[Combinatorial Reduction via Mask-Guided Residual Synthesis]
\label{prop:residual_decomp}
Let $|\mathcal{O}|$ denote the cardinality of parameterized base operators and $d$ the composition depth. Unconstrained joint synthesis of a depth-$(d+1)$ program requires evaluating $\Theta(|\mathcal{O}|^{d+1})$ candidates across all $K$ pairs. By freezing the elite depth-$d$ macro-program $h_{\text{st}}$ and restricting Cycle-3 residual operators $\Delta_{\text{res}} \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{res}}$ to act exclusively on local neighborhoods $\mathcal{N}_r(r,c)$ where discrepancy features align across $\{M_{\text{diff}}^{(k)}\}_{k=1}^K$:
\begin{equation}
\hat{Y}_{\text{final},k} = \hat{Y}_{\text{st},k} \oplus \Delta_{\text{res}}\big(X_k, \hat{Y}_{\text{st},k}; \Theta_{\text{local}}\big), \qquad \Theta_{\text{local}} = \text{InduceTable}\Big(\big\{(X_k, \hat{Y}_{\text{st},k}, Y_k)\big\}_{k=1}^K\Big),
\end{equation}
the synthesis complexity reduces from $\Theta(|\mathcal{O}|^{d+1})$ to $\Theta(|\mathcal{O}|^d + |\mathcal{P}_{\text{res}}|)$, where $|\mathcal{P}_{\text{res}}| \ll |\mathcal{O}|$ is evaluated deterministically via vectorized lookup tables in $O(\sum_k |Y_k|)$ time.
\end{proposition}

\subsection{Expected Free Energy ($\mathcal{G}_{F3}$) in $\mathbb{R}^{512}$ and Quadratic RHAE Regret Bounds}

In interactive $64\times 64$ grid environments (action space $\mathcal{A} = \{\text{UP}, \text{DOWN}, \text{LEFT}, \text{RIGHT}, \text{WAIT}, \text{CLICK}(r,c)\}$), each observed frame $O_t \in \mathbb{Z}_{16}^{64\times 64}$ is mapped by \texttt{AlgebraicWorldModelSLS} via a fixed-seed column-normalized random projection and $\ell_2$-sphere normalization into a $512\text{D}$ state representation $\mathbf{z}_t = \phi_{\text{SLS}}(O_t) \in \mathbb{R}^{512}$ (an analytical training-free projection inspired by \emph{SIGReg-JEPA} isotropic geometry \cite{balestriero2025lejepa,arnez2026sigreg}). Given visited latent memory $\mathcal{B}_t = \{\mathbf{z}_0, \dots, \mathbf{z}_t\}$, candidate actions $a \in \mathcal{A}$ minimize the \textbf{Complete Expected Free Energy}:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:efe_gf3}
\mathcal{G}_{F3}(a \mid O_t) = \underbrace{d_{\text{eff}}\big(\hat{O}_{t+1}(a),\, O_{\text{goal}}\big)}_{\text{Pragmatic Goal Divergence}} - \underbrace{\alpha_{\text{ep}} \cdot \min_{\mathbf{z}' \in \mathcal{B}_t} \|\hat{\mathbf{z}}_{t+1}(a) - \mathbf{z}'\|_2}_{\text{Epistemic State-Coverage (\texttt{PairDist})}} + \underbrace{c_{\text{ATP}}(a)}_{\text{Action Cost}} + \underbrace{\Psi_{\text{dead}}(\sigma(O_t), a)}_{\text{Dead-Signature Barrier}},
\end{equation}
where $\Psi_{\text{dead}}(\sigma(O_t), a) = \infty \cdot \mathbb{I}\big((\sigma(O_t), a_{\text{class}}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{dead}}\big)$ permanently blocks any state-action signature that yielded zero state transition ($\hat{O}_{t+1} = O_t$) and transfers causal object-color affordances across levels.

\begin{theorem}[Quadratic RHAE Regret Elimination via Dead-Signature Pruning]
\label{thm:rhae}
Consider an $L$-level interactive game where level $l \in \{1,\dots,L\}$ has human baseline action count $H_l$, $N_{\text{dist},l}$ inert distractor objects of color class $c_{\text{dist}}$, and requires $H_l$ productive actions to reach the goal.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item[(a)] An epistemic agent without Dead-Signature Pruning ($\Psi_{\text{dead}} \equiv 0$) that probes each unvisited object signature $m \ge 1$ times expends $A_l^{\text{no-dead}} = H_l + m\,N_{\text{dist},l}$ actions, suffering a quadratic RHAE score decay:
    \begin{equation}
    \text{RHAE}_l(\Psi_{\text{dead}} \equiv 0) = \left(\frac{H_l}{H_l + m\,N_{\text{dist},l}}\right)^2 \le \frac{1}{\big(1 + m\,N_{\text{dist},l}/H_l\big)^2}.
    \end{equation}
    \item[(b)] Under \textbf{F3-AGI v4.0 Dead-Signature Pruning and Cross-Level Rule Transfer}, any action $a$ that produces $O_{t+1} = O_t$ is added to $\mathcal{D}_{\text{dead}}$ immediately (limiting any unknown state-action signature to at most $1$ exploratory trial), and once color class $c_{\text{dist}}$ fails to alter state across $\tau_{\text{dead}}$ probes, all objects of color $c_{\text{dist}}$ are marked inert across subsequent levels $l=1,\dots,L$. Consequently, cumulative wasted probes over $L$ levels are bounded by $W_{\text{total}} \le \tau_{\text{dead}}$ in cold start, and $W_l = 0$ whenever static border/distractor kinematics are pre-registered in $\mathcal{D}_{\text{dead}}$ ($\tau_{\text{dead}}=0$), achieving $\text{RHAE}_l = \min(1, H_l/A_l^{\text{prod}})^2$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Unified Synaptic Plasticity Rule of \texttt{F3-Metabolic-Optim}}
\label{sec:math_metabolic}

Across sequential tasks $\mathcal{T}_1, \dots, \mathcal{T}_T$, let $\theta_l \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{\text{in}} \times d_{\text{out}}}$ denote the weights of layer $l$, $g_l(x, y) = \nabla_{\theta_l}\mathcal{L}(f_\theta(x), y)$ the instantaneous gradient, and $e(x, y) = 1 - p_\theta(y \mid x)$ the predictive margin error. The unified \textbf{F3-Metabolic-Optim} update is:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:f3_metabolic_optim}
\Delta \theta_l^{(t)} = -\eta \, \mathbb{I}_{\text{gate}}\big(x, y; \tau_m, \mathcal{M}_{\text{err}}\big) \, \mathbf{M}_l^{(t)} \odot \big(\mathbf{I} + \gamma_{\text{syn}} \hat{\mathbf{F}}_l^{(t)}\big)^{-1} \Big( g_l(x, y) + \lambda_{\text{anc}} \hat{\mathbf{F}}_l^{(t)} \odot (\theta_l^{(t)} - \theta_l^{*}) + \beta_{\text{MDL}}\,\text{sign}(\theta_l^{(t)}) \Big),
\end{equation}
where $\mathbb{I}_{\text{gate}}(x, y; \tau_m, \mathcal{M}_{\text{err}}) = \mathbb{I}\big( e(x, y) > \tau_m \lor (\text{id}(x) \in \mathcal{M}_{\text{err}} \land e(x, y) > 0.5\tau_m) \big)$ skips backward passes on mastered patterns ($\tau_m = 0.18$), $\hat{\mathbf{F}}_l^{(t)} = (1 - \rho)\hat{\mathbf{F}}_l^{(t-1)} + \rho\,(g_l(x,y))^{\odot 2}$ is the online \emph{SynGAP} diagonal Fisher EMA ($\gamma_{\text{syn}} = 12.0, \lambda_{\text{anc}} = 4.5$), and $\mathbf{M}_l^{(t)} \in [E_{\min}, E_{\max}]$ is the Mitochondrial ATP Tensor governed by Attwell--Laughlin depletion:
\begin{equation}
M_{l,j}^{(t+1)} = \text{clip}\!\Big( \big(M_{l,j}^{(t)} + r_{\text{base}}\,\Delta t\big)\exp(-\lambda_{\text{dep}}\,\nu_{l,j}\,\Delta t) - \alpha_{\text{cost}}\|\Delta\theta_{l,\cdot,j}^{(t)}\|_1,\; E_{\min},\; E_{\max} \Big).
\end{equation}

\section{Threats to Validity, Limitations, and Hybrid Wake-Sleep Roadmap}
% ============================================================================

\textbf{Threats to Validity: Separating Library Expressivity ($38.0\%$) from Strictly Uninspected Generalization ($10.86\%$).}
Scientific integrity in ARC-AGI research requires distinguishing between tasks whose training demonstrations informed library design and tasks that were never inspected in any form:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Expressivity vs.\ True Out-of-Distribution Generalization:} While we never inspected test outputs at any stage, the 10 new operators added in Horizon 5 (Table \ref{tab:nearmiss}) were engineered after inspecting the \emph{training pairs} of near-miss tasks within the first 50 tasks ($N=50$). Consequently, the $19/50$ ($38.0\%$) solve rate on the $N=50$ slice measures the \textbf{expressive ceiling} of the 30-operator DSL on that subset rather than pure zero-shot generalization. The rigorous, unbiased measure of zero-shot generalization is the performance on the \textbf{350 strictly uninspected evaluation tasks (tasks 51--400)}, where F3-AGI v4.0 solved \textbf{38 / 350 tasks (10.86\%)} (\textbf{57 / 400 = 14.25\%} overall).
    \item \emph{Critical Assessment of \texttt{attempt\_2} Ranking:} Across the 400 evaluation tasks, \texttt{pass@1} achieved $56/400$ ($14.00\%$) and \texttt{pass@2} achieved $57/400$ ($14.25\%$). The single task rescued exclusively by \texttt{attempt\_2} (\texttt{9a4bb226}) occurred because \texttt{attempt\_1} selected the largest connected object while \texttt{attempt\_2} selected the smallest object (which had a training score of only $0.30$). Thus, this $+1$ rescue represents incidental candidate diversity rather than systematic rank-2 calibration.
    \item \emph{Overfitting Rate on Solved Training Tasks:} Across all $400$ tasks, $61$ tasks achieved $\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h) = 1.0$ on all training pairs, and $53$ of those $61$ ($86.89\%$) transferred with $100\%$ exact accuracy to the unseen test grid (with $3$ additional tasks solved via near-1.0 candidates), confirming that $\mathcal{F}_{F3}$ suppresses false-positive overfitting across $86.9\%$ of exact training fits.
    \item \emph{Scope of Current Modules (Public Evaluation Split, Analytical $512\text{D}$ Projection, and Synthetic Continual Stream):} First, our 400-task benchmark evaluates the public \texttt{ARC-AGI/data/evaluation} corpus locally rather than the private server-side ARC Prize competition split. Second, \texttt{AlgebraicWorldModelSLS} in Study 3 uses a fixed-seed $\ell_2$-normalized random projection into $\mathbb{R}^{512}$ rather than a neural JEPA encoder trained end-to-end with the SIGReg objective. Third, Study 1 evaluates \texttt{F3-Metabolic-Optim} on a controlled $64\text{D}$ synthetic class-incremental stream, while Study 3 evaluates a local 5-game interactive grid suite using an RHAE-style efficiency metric.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Roadmap: Zero-Shot Evaluation on ARC-AGI-2 and Hybrid Wake-Sleep LoRA Consolidation.}
Because our pure-NumPy symbolic engine sweeps all 400 evaluation tasks in $10.8\,\text{min}$ ($1.62\,\text{s/task}$) and brings $128/400$ tasks ($32.0\%$) to $\ge 90\%$ test pixel accuracy, our immediate next steps are: (i) executing a zero-change blind evaluation on the \textbf{ARC-AGI-2} public evaluation set, and (ii) using the $1.62\,\text{s/task}$ symbolic engine as a fast \textbf{Wake Filter} that passes the top-1 partial program $\hat{Y}_{\text{st}}$ and its discrepancy mask $M_{\text{diff}}$ on the remaining tasks to a local \textbf{NREM Wake-Sleep LoRA Mutator} regulated by \texttt{F3-Metabolic-Optim}.

\textbf{Proof of Proposition \ref{prop:rashomon} (Rashomon Sublevel Set Collapse under $\mathcal{F}_{F3}$).}
Consider a task $\mathcal{D}_{\text{train}} = \{(X_k, Y_k)\}_{k=1}^K$ where each target grid $Y_k$ has background color $c_{\text{bg}}$, foreground pixel set $\Omega_{\text{fg}}^{(k)} = \{(r,c) : Y_{k,r,c} \neq c_{\text{bg}}\}$ with average foreground density $\rho_{\text{fg}} = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{|\Omega_{\text{fg}}^{(k)}|}{|Y_k|} \le 0.15$, at least one foreground object ($N_{\text{cc}}(Y_k) \ge 1$), and at least two distinct colors ($|C(Y_k)| \ge 2$).
For the constant background hypothesis $h_{\text{bg}}(X_k) = c_{\text{bg}}\mathbf{1}_{H_k \times W_k}$, the raw pixel loss is:
\begin{equation}
\ell_{\text{px}}(h_{\text{bg}}; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) = \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^K \frac{|\Omega_{\text{fg}}^{(k)}|}{|Y_k|} = \rho_{\text{fg}}.
\end{equation}
Thus, for any $\epsilon \ge \rho_{\text{fg}}$, $h_{\text{bg}} \in \mathcal{S}_\epsilon(\ell_{\text{px}})$. Now evaluate $\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h_{\text{bg}}; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}})$ under Equation (\ref{eq:structural_acc}):
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $S_{\text{shape}}(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k), Y_k) = 1.0$, contributing $w_{\text{sh}}(1.0) = 0.20$.
    \item On $\Omega_{\text{fg}}^{(k)}$, $h_{\text{bg}}(X_k)_{r,c} = c_{\text{bg}} \neq Y_{k,r,c}$, so foreground accuracy is $0.0$; on $\Omega_{\text{bg}}^{(k)}$, accuracy is $1.0$. By Equation (\ref{eq:s_pixel}), $S_{\text{pixel}}(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k), Y_k) = 0.70(0.0) + 0.30(1.0) = 0.30$ independently of $\rho_{\text{fg}}$, contributing $w_{\text{px}}(0.30) = 0.15$.
    \item Since $C(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k)) = \{c_{\text{bg}}\}$ and $|C(Y_k)| \ge 2$, the palette Jaccard similarity is $\frac{1}{|C(Y_k)|} \le \frac{1}{2}$. Since $N_{\text{cc}}(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k)) = 0$ and $N_{\text{cc}}(Y_k) \ge 1$, the component similarity is $\exp(-N_{\text{cc}}(Y_k)/N_{\text{cc}}(Y_k)) = e^{-1}$. By Equation (\ref{eq:s_object}), $S_{\text{object}}(h_{\text{bg}}(X_k), Y_k) \le 0.50(0.50) + 0.50 e^{-1} = 0.25 + 0.50 e^{-1}$, contributing at most $0.30(0.25 + 0.50 e^{-1}) = 0.075 + 0.15 e^{-1}$.
\end{enumerate}
Summing these three contributions gives $\mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h_{\text{bg}}) \le 0.425 + 0.15 e^{-1} \approx 0.4802$, and hence $\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h_{\text{bg}}) = 1 - \mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h_{\text{bg}}) \ge 0.575 - 0.15 e^{-1} \approx 0.5198$. For any exact program $h^\star$ with $\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h^\star) = 0$ and $\text{len}_{\text{AST}}(h^\star) \le L_{\max}$, $\mathcal{F}_{F3}(h^\star) \le \beta_{\text{MDL}} + \lambda_{\text{ATP}} c_{\text{tier}}(h^\star)/\bar{E}_{\text{ATP}}$, whereas $\mathcal{F}_{F3}(h_{\text{bg}}) \ge 0.5198 + \lambda_{\text{ATP}} c_{\text{tier}}(h_{\text{bg}})/\bar{E}_{\text{ATP}}$. Defining $\Delta_{\text{ATP}}(h^\star, h_{\text{bg}}) = \lambda_{\text{ATP}}(c_{\text{tier}}(h^\star) - c_{\text{tier}}(h_{\text{bg}}))/\bar{E}_{\text{ATP}}$ and subtracting yields $\mathcal{F}_{F3}(h_{\text{bg}}) - \mathcal{F}_{F3}(h^\star) \ge 0.5198 - 0.05 - \Delta_{\text{ATP}} = 0.4698 - \Delta_{\text{ATP}}$. When evaluated in the same tier ($c_{\text{tier}}(h^\star) = c_{\text{tier}}(h_{\text{bg}})$), the metabolic terms cancel identically ($\Delta_{\text{ATP}} = 0$), yielding $0.4698$; across different tiers ($\lambda_{\text{ATP}}=0.05, \Delta c_{\text{tier}} \le 0.04, \bar{E}_{\text{ATP}} \ge 0.10$), $\Delta_{\text{ATP}} \le 0.020$, preserving a certified gap $\ge 0.4498 > 0$. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{Proof of Lemma \ref{lemma:smallest_pair} (Smallest-Pair Monotone Shape Gate).}
If $\text{shape}(h(X_{k^*})) \neq \text{shape}(Y_{k^*})$ on the smallest pair $k^* = \arg\min_k |X_k|$, then by the piecewise definition of $S_{\text{shape}}$ and Eq.~\eqref{eq:s_pixel} on mismatched dimensions, $S_{\text{shape}}(\hat{Y}_{k^*}, Y_{k^*}) \le 0.25$ (incurring a shape error of at least $0.75\,w_{\text{sh}} = 0.15$) and $S_{\text{pixel}}(\hat{Y}_{k^*}, Y_{k^*}) = 0$ (incurring the full pixel weight loss $1.00\,w_{\text{px}} = 0.50$). Since every other pair $k \neq k^*$ has structural accuracy at most $1.0$, averaging across all $K$ pairs yields $\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) = 1 - \mathcal{A}_{\text{struct}}(h; \mathcal{D}_{\text{train}}) \ge \frac{0.15 + 0.50}{K} = \frac{0.65}{K} > 0$. Because any exact solution requires $\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}}(h) = 0$, $h$ cannot belong to $\mathcal{S}_0(\mathcal{E}_{\text{pred}})$ and is soundly pruned after $O(|X_{k^*}|)$ operations. $\blacksquare$

\textbf{Proof of Theorem \ref{thm:rhae} (Quadratic RHAE Regret Elimination).}
In level $l$, let $H_l$ be the human baseline action count and $A_l$ the agent's action count. When the level is completed, the official ARC-AGI-3 score is $\text{RHAE}_l = \min(1.0, (H_l/A_l)^2)$. Without Dead-Signature Pruning ($\Psi_{\text{dead}} \equiv 0$), an epistemic agent maximizing $\text{PairDist}$ in Equation (\ref{eq:efe_gf3}) assigns positive epistemic value to unprobed distractor objects $d \in \{1,\dots,N_{\text{dist},l}\}$ because its internal transition model initially predicts a novel latent state $\hat{\mathbf{z}}_{t+1}$. Probing each of the $N_{\text{dist},l}$ distractors $m \ge 1$ times adds $m\,N_{\text{dist},l}$ non-transitioning actions to the $H_l$ productive actions, giving $A_l = H_l + m\,N_{\text{dist},l}$ and $\text{RHAE}_l = (H_l / (H_l + m\,N_{\text{dist},l}))^2$. With \texttt{DeadSignatureTracker} ($\Psi_{\text{dead}} = \infty$ on $(\sigma(O_t), a_{\text{class}}) \in \mathcal{D}_{\text{dead}}$) and color-separated object extraction, any no-op action is immediately barred from re-selection, and static distractor color classes ($c \in \{5, 7\}$) are pruned globally across levels, yielding $W_l = 0$ wasted dead actions across all 15 levels. $\blacksquare$
